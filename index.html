References

Light, wardrobe and camera to follow
0 / 6

Locked rulesApplied to every prompt
  • Locked style. Professional photography, clean natural light, native 4K.
  • Look. Bold & colourful: Gen Z streetwear in saturated colour-block, low-angle wide camera, blue sky, hard sun, friends mid-laugh. Or Elegant & calm: tailored, neutral tones.
  • Wardrobe. Modern and contemporary. No hijab, no headscarf, no traditional Arab clothing. No orange clothing on anyone.
  • Modern Jordan. Today's Amman only: Abdali, new towers, sleek cafés, modern homes and offices. Nothing old or traditional.
  • Skin. Professional retouch: even skin tone, soft glow, subtle real texture, never plastic.
  • Phones. Always a modern iPhone 18, logo hidden.
  • Decor. Modern Scandinavian, IKEA style: light oak, white walls, clean lines, no clutter.
  • Composition lock. The person or group is always centred.
  • Studio lock. Studio puts the shot on a BAE studio set, whatever place the brief names.
  • BAE principles. Depth over flatness, clarity through focus, colour with warmth.
  • Never. Logos, readable text, watermarks, staged stock poses, heavy filters.
